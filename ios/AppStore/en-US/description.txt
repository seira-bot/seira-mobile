Your Seira bots work on your computer. When you step away from your desk, you can keep up with them from your iPhone or iPad: read their replies, answer their questions, and allow the steps that matter.

What you can do
• See every bot on one screen. Chats that need you come first.
• Allow or deny a step when a bot asks before it acts.
• Read replies while the bot writes them, with tables and formatted text.
• While Seira runs, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island shows the bot that needs you. You can answer some requests right from it.
• Add a widget to your Home Screen to see the chats that need you.
• Give each bot its own face: pick a Seira character and change its hair, face, extras, and color, or upload your own picture.
• Send a message or a new task. Type it, or dictate it.
• When a bot asks for an API key, fill it from Apple Passwords. The key goes to your computer encrypted and never shows in the chat.
• Share a link, a photo, or a document from another app to a bot.
• Search your chats, and share a chat as Markdown.
• Pair more than one computer and switch between them.
• Use the light or the dark look.

Before you start
Seira for iPhone and iPad is a companion to Seira on your computer. It does not run bots by itself.
• Install Seira on your Mac, Windows, or Linux computer and set up at least one bot.
• Your phone must reach that computer: on the same Wi-Fi network, through Tailscale, or at a secure address that Seira shows.
• iOS 16 or later.

How to pair
1. On your computer, open Seira, then Settings, then Phone & devices.
2. Open Seira on your iPhone or iPad and scan the QR code.
3. Check the computer name, then confirm.

Your bots stay on your computer. Your phone shows their work and sends your answers back. The app has no account, no ads, and no analytics.
