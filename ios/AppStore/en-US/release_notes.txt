A new look for Seira. Chats groups your bots by department, as on your computer. Fold a department, add bots to it, or make a new bot right inside it.

Turn on Advanced mode in Settings for more tools: rename a chat, copy it or save it as Markdown, see how full the context is and compact it, and allow a step for the rest of a session.

Automations asks "What should happen?" and sets up the routine for you. The calendar has a Week view.

Some features need the newest Seira on your computer.
